  • To InRule: from other rule engines (IBM ODM / ILOG, FICO Blaze Advisor, Drools), from rules hard-coded in applications or stored procedures, and from spreadsheets. We extract the logic, rebuild it as rule applications, and prove the result against the old system with the same test cases.

  • From InRule: to DMN models running in Drools or Camunda, or to plain C#, when your architecture is moving that way. Including the parts automatic converters leave to you: bound .NET methods, rules embedded in Word documents, calculations InRule re-evaluates automatically, and parent-context references. Same method in reverse: the existing rule applications are the specification, and both sides run the same tests before anything switches over.

  • Between InRule versions and hosting models: irServer to Decision Services, on-premises to Azure, and catalog consolidation.

Questions

Are you part of InRule?

No. Geny Systems is an independent company. We work with InRule software on our clients' licences.

Can we see the tools before a call?

Yes. The demo above is public, and on a call we show Spec Builder live, producing DMN and C#. The InRule version is installed only for clients, under a signed agreement, in their own environment and on their own InRule licence.

Whose InRule licence is used for the work?

Yours. All InRule work runs on your licence, in your environment. Geny Systems does not supply or resell InRule licences.

Does the AI change our rules on its own?

No. The AI proposes a change, your regression tests run against it, and a person on your team approves it before it is deployed. The rule engine still makes every decision.

Which AI assistants and models does it work with?

Any assistant that supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), including Claude and GitHub Copilot, plus the assistant inside irAuthor. It uses the AI model provider you approve, for example Azure OpenAI in your own Azure tenant.

Which InRule versions do you work with?

Current 5.x releases, including the move from irServer to Decision Services. Tell us your version on the first call.

How do you migrate off InRule?

We export the rule applications and their test scenarios, rebuild them in DMN or C#, and run the same tests on both sides. We switch over only when every test passes on the new side.

Can you work inside our network?

Yes, through your virtual desktop or VPN, under your access rules. We sign your NDA or ours.

Where are you based, and in which time zone?

Bucharest, Romania (EET, UTC+2; UTC+3 in summer). We work remotely and travel for workshops and on-site phases.

How fast can you start?

Usually within two weeks of a signed proposal.

Can we hire an InRule consultant for a short task?

Yes. A fix, a review, a new rule set or a few days of help before a release are all fine. We work by the hour or at a fixed price for the task.

Have an InRule problem on your list?

Twenty minutes is enough to tell whether we can help, and what it would cost.
