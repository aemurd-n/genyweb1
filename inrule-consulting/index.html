What we do

Rule application development

New rule applications, and changes to the ones you have: entities, rule sets, decision tables, calculations, validations, vocabulary. Built in irAuthor and through the InRule SDK, tested before they ship.

Rule application reviews

A one-week review of your rule application portfolio: complexity, test coverage, risky logic, performance, dead rules and documentation gaps, with a prioritised list of what to fix first.

Version upgrades and Decision Services

Release 5.9.0 retired the classic irServer Rule Execution Service. We move rule execution to Decision Services, and upgrade rule applications and integrations to current InRule versions, with a regression baseline before and after.

Documentation of inherited rule applications

Plain-English documentation of every rule set, decision maps, dependencies, a field and value glossary, and a change-impact matrix, for rule applications whose authors have moved on.

Regression test suites

Test suites that capture what your rule applications decide today, so every change and every upgrade is checked before it reaches production. You keep the suite and the harness.

Ongoing rule changes

A monthly retainer for rule authoring, changes and testing, with an agreed response time, for teams without an InRule specialist in house.

Training

On-site or remote training for rule authors and developers, from first rule application to SDK integration.

Custom tools and extensions

InRule is built to be extended. We build the pieces your teams are missing:

  • irAuthor extensions: panels, ribbon commands, checks and helpers inside the authoring tool your authors already use.

  • SDK tooling: generators, validators, bulk changes, reports and analysis across many rule applications at once.

  • Integrations: calling rules from your applications and services, REST endpoints, data mapping, and integration with the systems around your decisions.

  • Testing and documentation tooling: test generation, test runners in your CI pipeline, documentation produced from the rule applications themselves.

Where it helps, we use AI-assisted tooling to produce documentation and test suites faster, always reviewed by us and run on your side.

  • To InRule: from other rule engines (IBM ODM / ILOG, FICO Blaze Advisor, Drools), from rules hard-coded in applications or stored procedures, and from spreadsheets. We extract the logic, rebuild it as rule applications, and prove the result against the old system with the same test cases.

  • From InRule: to DMN models running in Drools or Camunda, or to plain C#, when your architecture is moving that way. Same method in reverse: the existing rule applications are the specification, and both sides run the same tests before anything switches over.

  • Between InRule versions and hosting models: irServer to Decision Services, on-premises to Azure, and catalog consolidation.

Questions

Are you part of InRule?

No. Geny Systems is an independent company. We work with InRule software on our clients' licences.

Whose InRule licence is used for the work?

Yours. All InRule work runs on your licence, in your environment. Geny Systems does not supply or resell InRule licences.

Which InRule versions do you work with?

Current 5.x releases, including the move from irServer to Decision Services. Tell us your version on the first call.

Can you work inside our network?

Yes, through your virtual desktop or VPN, under your access rules. We sign your NDA or ours.

Where are you based, and in which time zone?

Bucharest, Romania (EET, UTC+2; UTC+3 in summer). We work remotely and travel for workshops and on-site phases.

How fast can you start?

Usually within two weeks of a signed proposal.
