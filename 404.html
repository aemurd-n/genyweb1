Production AI, built for the real world

Geny Systems is a product-led AI company that builds and operates a portfolio of production AI applications across finance, healthcare and enterprise automation. We run our own products in production on reusable, model-agnostic AI infrastructure for agents, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), document intelligence and automation. Geny Systems is a member of the Anthropic Claude Partner Network and builds on Microsoft Azure AI Foundry.

Our products

eDecont.ro: AI expense automation for Romanian businesses

Expense reports, receipts and approvals in one place. AI reads any receipt or PDF and extracts vendor, amounts, VAT and line items with confidence scoring. eDecont.ro also syncs e-Factura, validates vendors against ANAF (the Romanian tax authority), handles multi-currency at BNR rates, runs multi-level expense approval workflows with per-diem, reconciles bank statements, and generates fiscal exports (SAF-T, D300, D390, Nota Contabila) plus accounting exports to Saga, SmartBill, QuickBooks, Excel and CSV. Capture receipts from your phone as a PWA or forward them by email. Built for Romanian businesses.

genyHealth.ai: longitudinal health & genomics intelligence

AI analyzes your DNA once and genyHealth watches it for life, connecting genetics, labs, wearables (Garmin, Fitbit and Oura), medications and emerging research into one private, continuously updated health intelligence layer. It adds pharmacogenomics, polygenic risk scores, an AI health chat grounded in your own data, physician-ready PDF reports with FHIR export, and PubMed and ClinVar research matching to your specific variants. Currently available to invited members.

Enterprise decision automation (in private development)

A third, more technically complex product is still in private development: an AI platform for enterprise decision automation that turns natural-language requirements into validated, executable business logic (rules, decision tables and test suites) through an agentic architecture built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

What we build

LLM-based chatbots and copilots, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) grounded in your proprietary data, MCP tools and servers, autonomous AI agents, fine-tuned and self-hosted models, document intelligence, and end-to-end automation. Model-agnostic across Anthropic Claude, Azure AI Foundry, OpenAI and open models: the right engine for each problem, with no vendor lock-in.
