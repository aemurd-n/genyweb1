Free startup credits: a checked list

Cloud, AI and developer-tool programmes that give early-stage software companies free credits or free plans. Each entry shows what is offered, who can apply, how to apply and the deadline, in plain terms.

List updated 20 October 2026

How this list is checked

  • Every entry comes from the programme's own official page, never from a search result, a reseller or another list.
  • Each entry links to the official page it was checked on, so you can confirm the terms yourself.
  • Each entry shows the date it was last checked. Programmes change their terms often; an entry is re-checked when it is more than 30 days old.
  • A programme is left off the list when its official page does not state what it offers, who can apply and how to apply, or when it is closed.
  • Amounts and conditions are copied as the provider states them. Where a page leaves something out, such as a credit amount or an age limit, the entry says so instead of guessing.
