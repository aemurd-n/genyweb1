Our products

eDecont.ro: AI receipt scanning & Romanian fiscal automation

AI-powered OCR reads any receipt or PDF and extracts vendor, amounts, VAT and line items with confidence scoring. eDecont.ro also syncs e-Factura, validates vendors against ANAF (the Romanian tax authority), handles multi-currency at BNR rates, runs multi-level expense approval workflows with per-diem, reconciles bank statements, and generates fiscal exports (SAF-T, D300, D390, Nota Contabila) plus accounting exports to Saga, SmartBill, QuickBooks, Excel and CSV. Capture receipts from your phone as a PWA or forward them by email. Built for Romanian businesses.

genyHealth.ai: longitudinal health & genomics intelligence

AI analyzes your DNA once and genyHealth watches it for life, connecting genetics, labs, wearables (Garmin, Fitbit and Oura), medications and emerging research into one private, continuously updated health intelligence layer. It adds pharmacogenomics, polygenic risk scores, an AI health chat grounded in your own data, physician-ready PDF reports with FHIR export, and PubMed and ClinVar research matching to your specific variants. Currently available to invited members and selected clinical partners.

Enterprise decision-automation platform — in private development

The majority of our engineering effort goes into a third, more technically complex product still in private development: an AI platform for enterprise decision automation that turns natural-language requirements into validated, executable business logic — rules, decision tables and test suites — through an agentic, Model-Context-Protocol (MCP) based architecture.

LLM-based chatbots and copilots, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) grounded in your proprietary data, MCP tools and servers, autonomous AI agents, fine-tuned and self-hosted models, document intelligence, and end-to-end automation. Model-agnostic across Anthropic Claude, Azure AI Foundry, OpenAI and open models — the right engine for each problem, with no vendor lock-in, and EU-hosted.
