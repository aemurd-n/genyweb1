About Geny Systems

One line

Geny Systems is an AI company from Romania that builds production AI applications for complex, regulated work, on one shared AI engine reused across domains.

Short description

Geny Systems is a product-led AI company based in Bucharest, Romania. It builds and operates production AI applications on one shared, model-agnostic AI and application platform. Its live products are eDecont.ro, which automates expense reports, receipt and invoice processing and Romanian fiscal compliance, and genyHealth.ai, which turns lab results, biomarkers, wearables and optional genetic data into one longitudinal health record with personalised, plain-language insights.
