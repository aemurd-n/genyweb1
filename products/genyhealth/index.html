Frequently Asked Questions

What genetic data can I upload?

genyHealth supports raw DNA files from 23andMe and AncestryDNA. Your genome is analyzed once and re-read for life as research evolves.

Which wearables does genyHealth connect to?

Garmin, Fitbit and Oura, covering sleep, activity, stress, heart rate variability and VO2 max, alongside your genetics and labs.

Is my health data private?

Yes. genyHealth is built privacy-first with granular consent, GDPR data export and deletion, and encryption. Your data is never sold or shared without your explicit consent.

What is pharmacogenomics and does genyHealth cover it?

Pharmacogenomics studies how your genes affect your response to medications. genyHealth surfaces drug-gene context so you and your physician can make better-informed choices.

What are polygenic risk scores?

Polygenic risk scores combine many genetic variants into a single estimate of your genetic risk for a condition, going beyond single-gene testing and adjusted for lifestyle factors.

Can I share results with my doctor?

Yes. genyHealth generates physician-ready PDF summaries with citations and can export your data in FHIR format for clinical systems.

How do I get access?

genyHealth is currently available to invited members. Request access and we will be in touch.
