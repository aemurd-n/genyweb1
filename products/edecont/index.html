Key Features

Capture

Receipts and invoices in, from any device, in any currency.

Mobile Capture

Frequently Asked Questions

Does eDecont.ro support e-Factura?

Yes. eDecont syncs inbound e-Factura invoices, lets you upload your own, and exports compliant XML, so you meet the Romanian e-invoicing mandate without leaving the app.

Can it generate SAF-T and VAT returns?

Yes. eDecont produces SAF-T (D406) files and prepares D300 and D390 VAT return data, along with Notă Contabilă double-entry exports for your accountant.

How accurate is the receipt scanning?

Vision AI extracts vendor, amounts, VAT and line items from photos and PDFs, and shows a confidence score on every field so you can review anything uncertain. Multi-page and scanned PDFs are supported.

Which accounting software can I export to?

Saga, SmartBill and QuickBooks, plus Excel and CSV. Notă Contabilă exports cover double-entry accounting.

Does it check vendors with ANAF?

Yes. eDecont looks up the CUI against the ANAF registry and verifies vendor status, VAT registration and company details in real time.

Do I need to install an app?

No. eDecont works from your phone browser as a PWA, and you can forward receipts to a private email address for automatic import.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. eDecont offers a free tier to get started, with paid plans priced per active user. See current plans on eDecont.ro.
